# Call scripts for recording (10 calls)

All names, amounts and details are made up. Two people record each script, one as the Agent and one as the Borrower, on a phone or laptop microphone. Read naturally at normal speed, in your own voice. Do not use text-to-speech. Save each recording as call_01.wav to call_10.wav.

Note: the scripts for calls 06 to 10 were added after the first five and supply the intended dialogue for those recordings. The recordings have not been verified word-for-word against them.

Do not look at the answer key before you finish your system.

---

## call_01 (Hindi)

**Agent:** Namaste, main Pooja Rane bol rahi hoon, Shaha Finlease se. Kya meri baat Dinesh Kale ji se ho rahi hai?
**Borrower:** Ji haan, main Dinesh bol raha hoon.
**Agent:** Dhanyavaad Dinesh ji. Yeh call recorded hai. Main aapke personal loan ke baare mein baat karna chahti thi. Kya aap abhi baat kar sakte hain?
**Borrower:** Haan, boliye.
**Agent:** Aapke loan account mein abhi 48,000 rupaye baaki hain. Kya aap bata sakte hain ki payment mein kya dikkat aa rahi hai?
**Borrower:** Madam, pichle do mahine se meri naukri nahi hai. Isliye nahi de paaya.
**Agent:** Main samajh sakti hoon, yeh mushkil samay hai. Kya aap abhi kuch chhoti rakam de sakte hain, jaise 5,000 rupaye?
**Borrower:** Abhi mushkil hai. Agle mahine ki 10 tarikh tak 3,000 rupaye de sakta hoon.
**Agent:** Theek hai. Main note kar leti hoon, 10 tarikh tak 3,000 rupaye. Main aapko payment link bhej dungi.
**Borrower:** Ji, bhej dijiye.
**Agent:** Dhanyavaad Dinesh ji. Aapka din shubh ho.

## call_09 — Third-party disclosure (English)

**Agent:** Good afternoon, this is Preet Jhagekar calling from Shaha Finlease regarding Mr. Karan Malhotra. Am I speaking with someone from his office?
**Colleague:** Yes, I'm his colleague. May I know why you're calling?
**Agent:** I'm calling because Karan has an outstanding personal loan of 55,000 rupees with our company.
**Colleague:** I'm sorry, but I don't handle his personal matters. You should speak to him directly.
**Agent:** He has missed several payments, and we haven't received the amount despite multiple reminders.
**Colleague:** That's private information. Why are you sharing it with me?
**Agent:** Please ask him to contact Shaha Finlease as soon as possible about his pending payments.
**Colleague:** I'll tell him that your company called, but please don't share his financial details with other people.
**Agent:** Understood. Please ask him to contact us directly. Thank you for your time.
**Colleague:** All right. Goodbye.
